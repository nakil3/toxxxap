Stream Clip Publisher

A self-hosted tool for turning livestream highlights into short-form videos and publishing them to the creator's own social accounts.

What it does

Stream Clip Publisher records a livestream, detects moments where the chat reacts strongly, and automatically edits those moments into vertical short-form clips: 9:16 aspect ratio, webcam framing, and a generated caption burned into the video.

Finished clips are collected in a local dashboard. From there the operator reviews each clip, edits its caption and hashtags, chooses the audience and interaction settings, and publishes it to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Who uses it

The application is operated by an individual developer and is used only to publish to social media accounts that the operator personally owns. It is not a public service, it has no user registration, and it does not accept content from third parties.

Content ownership. All published material consists of short excerpts of livestreams that the operator has permission to clip and redistribute. No third-party content is uploaded through this application.

How TikTok is used

The application uses the TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. Connecting a TikTok account is initiated by the account owner through the standard TikTok authorization screen. Before any content is sent to TikTok, the operator sees a preview of the clip, its caption, the selected audience and the selected interaction settings, and confirms the publication explicitly.

The application never adds watermarks or logos to content, and never posts on behalf of an account without an explicit confirmation for that specific post.
